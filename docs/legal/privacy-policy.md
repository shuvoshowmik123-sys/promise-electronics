This policy explains what information we hold about you, why we hold it, who
else can see it, and how to have it removed. It is written plainly on purpose.

## 1. What we collect

### When you request a repair

- **Your name and phone number** — so we can reach you about your device
- **Your email address**, if you give one
- **Your address**, where you ask us to collect or deliver
- **The location pin you drop on the map**, if you use it — this is a precise
  GPS coordinate, and it is used only to help our driver find your door
- **Photographs and videos of your device** that you upload — the fault, the
  model, the serial plate
- **What you tell us about the fault** — brand, model, symptoms, history

### When you create an account

- Your phone number, name, and password (stored encrypted — we cannot read it)
- If you sign in with Google: your name, email address, and profile picture from
  your Google account. We do not receive your Google password.

### As your repair progresses

- The work carried out, parts fitted, and technician notes
- Photographs taken by our staff at collection and delivery, recording the
  condition of your device
- Payments, invoices, and warranty periods
- Delivery confirmations, including the time and the staff member involved

### Automatically

- Basic technical information from your browser or phone, so the website works
  and stays secure
- **Notification permission**, if you allow it, so we can tell you when your
  repair status changes. You can withdraw this at any time in your browser or
  phone settings.

## 2. Why we hold it

| Purpose | What we use |
|---|---|
| Carrying out your repair | Contact details, device details, fault description |
| Collecting and returning your device | Address, GPS pin, phone number |
| Proving the condition of your device | Photographs at each handover |
| Confirming handover securely | 6-digit code sent to your account |
| Sending you status updates | Phone number, email, notification permission |
| Honouring your warranty | Repair history, invoice, parts fitted |
| Meeting tax and accounting obligations | Invoices and payment records |

We do **not** sell your information. We do not share it for advertising.

## 3. Photographs and video

Photographs of your device are stored so that both you and we have a record of
its condition — before repair and at delivery. This protects you if something is
damaged, and protects us against claims about damage that was already there.

Please avoid photographing anything personal in the background. If you upload a
photograph by mistake, tell us and we will delete it.

## 4. Your location

If you drop a pin on the map, we store that coordinate against your request.

We use it once — to route our driver. **We do not track your location**, we do
not follow your device, and we collect nothing when you are not using the
website.

## 5. Where your device's own data is concerned

We do not copy, browse, or extract anything stored on your television — no
accounts, no recordings, no personal content.

Repair may require replacing components that hold settings or logged-in
accounts, so please back up anything important before handing over the device.

processed abroad. Each is a recognised provider with its own security and
privacy obligations.

## 7. Our use of AI

**We use AI tools to help run this business, and we would rather tell you than
have you find out.**

They help our staff with things like summarising a fault description,
suggesting a likely diagnosis, and drafting replies. Requests sent to these
tools may include the device details and fault description you gave us.

Two things we commit to:

- **A person makes the decision.** AI does not decide your price, approve your
  repair, or determine a warranty claim. A technician does.
- **We keep out what is not needed.** We do not send your photographs, address,
  GPS pin, or payment details to these tools.

## 8. How long we keep it

**We keep your repair records for 3 years.**

This covers the longest warranty we offer, gives you a service history if you
return, and satisfies our accounting obligations. Accounting records that law
requires us to retain for longer are kept for that period.

After that, records are deleted.

## 9. Your rights

You can ask us to:

- **Show you** what we hold about you
- **Correct** anything wrong — a misspelled name, an old address
- **Delete** your information
- **Stop messaging you** — we will still contact you about a repair currently in
  progress, because we need to
- **Close your account**

**On deletion:** we will delete your information when you ask. The one exception
is records the law requires us to keep — invoices and tax records, principally.
We will tell you exactly what is being kept and why.

To make any of these requests, call **+880 1944 88999** or visit the shop. We
respond within 30 days. There is no charge.

## 10. Keeping it safe

- Passwords are stored encrypted and cannot be read by our staff
- The website uses HTTPS, so information is encrypted in transit
- Staff access is limited by role — a driver sees the delivery address, not your
  payment history
- Database backups are encrypted
- Handover codes expire

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach ever affects your information, we
will tell you.

## 11. Handover codes

When our staff collect or deliver a device, we send a 6-digit code to your
account and you read it back to them.

**Never give this code to anyone over the phone.** Only to the person standing
in front of you, holding the device.

## 12. Cookies

We use a small number of cookies to keep you signed in and to keep the site
secure. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

## 13. Children

Our services are for adults. We do not knowingly collect information about
children. If a child's information has reached us, tell us and we will delete
it.

## 14. Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your information, we will update this page and change
the date at the top. Significant changes will be notified in the app.
