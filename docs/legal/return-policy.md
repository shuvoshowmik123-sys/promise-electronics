We want you to receive a part that works. If it does not, we will put it right.

## You have 3 days

You may return a part within 3 days of receiving it if it arrives with a
serious defect. The 3 days are counted from the day the part reaches you.

## What counts as a serious defect

- The panel shows distortion
- The panel is cracked, broken or damaged
- There are lines across the display
- The part does not work at all

If you have any of these, contact us within 3 days and we will replace the part
or refund you.

## What is not covered

**Dead or stuck pixels are not covered.** A small number of pixel faults is
normal in panel manufacturing and is not treated as a defect by the makers or
by us.

We also cannot accept a return when:

- The 3 days have passed
- The part was damaged during fitting or by incorrect installation
- The part was damaged by power surge, water, or physical impact after delivery
- You simply changed your mind. Electronic parts cannot be resold once handled,
  so we accept returns for defects only.

## How to return a part

1. Call or message us within 3 days
2. Tell us what is wrong and send a photo or a short video if you can
3. Bring the part to our service centre, or ask us to collect it

Please keep the part in its original condition and packaging where possible.

## Delivery cost on a return

If the part is genuinely defective, we cover the cost of collecting it and
delivering the replacement. Nothing is charged to you.

## Exchange or refund

Once we confirm the defect, you choose:

- **Exchange** — we send a replacement part
- **Refund** — we return your money by the method you paid with

We aim to settle this within 3 working days of receiving the part back.

## Questions

Call us before returning anything. Most problems are settled faster on the
phone, and we would rather help you fit the part correctly than take it back.
