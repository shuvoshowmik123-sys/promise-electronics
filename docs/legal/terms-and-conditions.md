## 2. What we do

- Diagnosis and repair of televisions and related electronics

## 3. Inspection is free

We inspect your device and give you a quotation at no charge. **You are under no
obligation to proceed.** If you decline the quotation, we return your device

Transport charges for collection and delivery are separate — see section 6.

## 4. Approving a repair

We begin work only after you approve the quotation.

If, once work has started, we find additional faults that change the price, we
stop and contact you. We do not carry out unapproved work and we do not charge
for work you have not agreed to.

## 5. Payment

Payment in full is due when you collect the device, or when we deliver it to

We accept cash and the mobile payment methods shown on your invoice. Your

## 6. Collection, delivery and cancellation

Where you ask us to collect your device, the transport charge is stated before
we send a driver.

**If you cancel after we have collected the device**, our collection charge
applies and a return delivery charge is added. Both journeys have been made, and
both are charged.

If you cancel before we collect, nothing is charged.

## 7. Confirming handover

When our staff collect or return a device, we send a **6-digit code** to your
account. You read this code to the staff member to confirm the handover.

This protects you: it means your television is only released to you, and only
collected with your knowledge. Please give the code only to the person standing
in front of you, and never over the phone.

If a code cannot be delivered, our staff will record the handover with a written
reason and a photograph instead.

## 8. Warranty

**The warranty for your repair is recorded on your job ticket and invoice.** It
varies by the type of work carried out. Standard periods are:

| Work carried out | Warranty |
|---|---|
| Display or panel replacement | 6 months |
| Panel repair | 60 days |
| Other repairs | As stated on your job ticket |

Where a part carries a manufacturer's or supplier's warranty, that warranty
applies to the part and is stated separately from our warranty on the labour.

### What the warranty covers

Recurrence of **the same fault** we repaired, within the stated period. We repair
it again at no charge.

### What the warranty does not cover

- A different fault, unrelated to the work we carried out
- Physical damage, impact, or a cracked screen occurring after collection
- Liquid damage, moisture, or insect ingress
- Damage from power surges, lightning, or unstable electricity supply
- Any device opened, modified or worked on by another technician after our repair
- Normal wear of components not replaced by us

Bringing the device to another repairer ends our warranty on that work, because
we can no longer know what was changed.
