1. WHO WE ARE

Promise Electronics is a television repair business operating as a sole
proprietorship in Dhaka, Bangladesh. In these terms, "we", "us" and "our"
mean Promise Electronics. "You" means the customer.

By sending us a repair request, handing us a device, or using our website,
you agree to these terms.


2. WHAT WE DO

  - Diagnosis and repair of televisions and related electronics
  - Collection from and delivery to your address, where you request it
  - Supply and fitting of replacement parts


3. INSPECTION IS FREE

We inspect your device and give you a quotation at no charge. You are under
no obligation to proceed. If you decline the quotation, we return your
device without charging an inspection fee.

Transport charges for collection and delivery are separate — see section 6.


4. APPROVING A REPAIR

We begin work only after you approve the quotation.

If, once work has started, we find additional faults that change the price,
we stop and contact you. We do not carry out unapproved work and we do not
charge for work you have not agreed to.


5. PAYMENT

Payment in full is due when you collect the device, or when we deliver it
to you.

We accept cash and the mobile payment methods shown on your invoice. Your
invoice records what you paid, when, and for what.


6. COLLECTION, DELIVERY AND CANCELLATION

Where you ask us to collect your device, the transport charge is stated
before we send a driver.

If you cancel after we have collected the device, our collection charge
applies and a return delivery charge is added. Both journeys have been
made, and both are charged.

If you cancel before we collect, nothing is charged.


7. CONFIRMING HANDOVER

When our staff collect or return a device, we send a 6-digit code to your
account. You read this code to the staff member to confirm the handover.

This protects you: it means your television is only released to you, and
only collected with your knowledge. Please give the code only to the person
standing in front of you, and never over the phone.

If a code cannot be delivered, our staff will record the handover with a
written reason and a photograph instead.


8. WARRANTY

The warranty for your repair is recorded on your job ticket and invoice.
It varies by the type of work carried out. Standard periods are:

  Display or panel replacement . . . . . . . . . 6 months
  Panel repair  . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . 60 days
  Other repairs . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . As stated on your
                                                  job ticket

Where a part carries a manufacturer's or supplier's warranty, that warranty
applies to the part and is stated separately from our warranty on the
labour.

What the warranty covers

  Recurrence of the same fault we repaired, within the stated period. We
  repair it again at no charge.

What the warranty does not cover

  - A different fault, unrelated to the work we carried out
  - Physical damage, impact, or a cracked screen occurring after collection
  - Liquid damage, moisture, or insect ingress
  - Damage from power surges, lightning, or unstable electricity supply
  - Any device opened, modified or worked on by another technician after
    our repair
  - Normal wear of components not replaced by us

Bringing the device to another repairer ends our warranty on that work,
because we can no longer know what was changed.


9. UNCOLLECTED DEVICES

When your repair is complete we notify you by SMS and telephone.

If your device remains uncollected 90 days after that notification, we may
sell or dispose of it to recover our repair and storage costs. We will
attempt to contact you before doing so.

Please collect your device promptly. We have limited storage space and
cannot hold devices indefinitely.


10. YOUR DATA ON THE DEVICE

Please back up anything you need before handing over your device.

Repair may require replacing components that hold settings, accounts or
stored content. We are not responsible for the loss of apps, accounts,
recordings, settings, or any other data on your device.

We do not access your personal content, and we do not copy anything from
your device.


11. IF WE DAMAGE OR LOSE YOUR DEVICE

If your device is damaged or lost while in our care, we will repair the
damage or provide a device of equivalent specification and condition.

We record the condition of your device when we receive it, including
photographs, so that both of us have a clear record.

Our responsibility does not extend to indirect losses — for example,
income lost because a television was unavailable.


12. YOUR RESPONSIBILITIES

  - Give us accurate contact details so we can reach you about your repair

  - Collect your device within a reasonable time
  - Do not present a device you are not entitled to hand over
