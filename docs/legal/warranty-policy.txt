THE SHORT VERSION

Your warranty is printed on your job ticket and invoice. It is not the same
for every repair, because a replaced panel and a repaired board are not the
same job. Check your ticket — that figure is the one that applies to you.


STANDARD PERIODS

  Display or panel replacement . . . . . . . . . 6 months
  Panel repair  . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . 60 days
  Board-level repair, power supply, backlight
  and other work  . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . As stated on your
                                                  job ticket

Where a part carries its own manufacturer's or supplier's warranty, that is
stated separately on your invoice. It applies to the part; ours applies to
the work.

The warranty starts on the day you collect the device or we deliver it, not
on the day you brought it in.


WHAT IS COVERED

The same fault coming back.

If the problem we repaired returns within the stated period, bring the
device in. We inspect it, and if it is the same fault, we repair it again —
no charge for parts or labour.


WHAT IS NOT COVERED

  - A different fault. Televisions have many components. A new, unrelated
    failure is a new repair.
  - Physical damage — impact, a cracked or pressed panel, damage in
    transport after we returned the device
  - Liquid, moisture, or insect ingress
  - Power problems — surges, lightning, unstable supply. Please use a
    stabiliser or surge protector.
  - Work by another technician. If the device has been opened or worked on
    by anyone else after our repair, we can no longer know what changed,
    and the warranty on that work ends.
  - Removed or altered warranty seal or service label
  - Normal wear of components we did not replace


HOW TO MAKE A CLAIM

  1. Call +880 1944 88999 or come to the shop
  2. Bring the device and your invoice or job ticket — or just give us your
     phone number, and we will find your record
  3. We inspect it and tell you whether it falls under the warranty, and
     why

We will always tell you the reason for a decision. If we conclude a fault
is not covered, we explain what we found and quote you for the repair
before doing anything.


COLLECTION AND DELIVERY ON A WARRANTY CLAIM

The repair itself is free under warranty. If you ask us to collect and
return the device rather than bringing it in, transport is charged at our
normal rate, because the driver's journey is a real cost separate from the
repair.


IF WE CANNOT REPAIR IT AGAIN

If the same fault returns and it cannot be repaired, we replace the part or
provide a device of equivalent specification and condition.


BACK UP YOUR DATA

A warranty repair may again require replacing components that hold settings
and signed-in accounts. Please back up anything you need beforehand.


TRANSFERRING THE WARRANTY

The warranty covers the device, not the person. If you sell or give away
the television, the remaining warranty goes with it — the new owner needs
the invoice or job ticket number.
